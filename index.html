Overview

Orioncor aims to transform the offshore oil and gas sector by developing an AI-powered cybersecurity platform tailored to protect exploration, production, and transportation systems operating in marine environments. Our mission is to strengthen the digital resilience of offshore operations through real-time threat detection, predictive analytics, and adaptive defense mechanisms. The platform will deliver a secure, scalable, and compliant solution that helps operators safeguard critical assets, minimize downtime, and ensure continuity of energy supply while meeting industry cybersecurity and safety standards.
